Three ways to work with us

Pick the one that fits where your product is today. Every option has a named team, a written scope and a weekly update.

Platform builds

Fixed price · 6–10 weeks

You bring the idea, we scope it with you and ship a working first version. Price and dates are agreed up front, so there are no open-ended invoices.

  • Scope, price and timeline in writing before we start
  • A working build to try every week
  • Launch on the App Store, Play Store or your own domain

Development retainers

Monthly · ongoing

Ongoing development that keeps your product moving after launch: new features, OS and framework upgrades, fixes and maintenance.

  • Agreed hours each month, planned with you
  • Same engineers month to month
  • Scope reviewed with you each month

Rescue work

For half-built products

Another team started it and didn't finish? We audit what's there, tell you plainly what to keep and what to fix, then get it to launch.

  • Written audit of the code and infrastructure
  • A fixed plan and price to reach launch
  • No rewrite unless the code really needs one

How a platform build runs

From first call to launch in 6–10 weeks. You see progress every week, not just at the end.

  1. Week 0

    Call and scope

    A 30-minute call about what you want to build and why. We follow up with a written scope, a fixed price and a launch date.

  2. Week 1

    Design

    Screens and flows for the first release, agreed with you before any code is written.

  3. Weeks 2–9

    Build in weekly releases

    A working build you can try every week, and a short update: what shipped, what's next, what's blocked.

  4. Weeks 6–10

    Launch and hand over

    We release to your users, hand over the code and accounts, and stay on for fixes. A retainer is there if you want one.
